filters (state / dateFrom / dateTo), the 5-arm sort selector, `<Link>`

empty-state variants, and the unauthenticated redirect.

- Confirmed customer with ≥ 3 orders in mixed states (incl. one with
  `unreadMessageCount > 0` and one custom order with `productTitle = null`).
- A second customer with ≥ 25 orders for the pagination walk.
- A fresh customer with zero orders for the empty state.
