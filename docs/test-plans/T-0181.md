## Correction to the ticket as written
The ticket claimed "no state-machine change needed". That was **wrong** and I corrected it while
building: a full `Refund()` lands the order in `Refunded`, but the answer asked for the order to be
**cancelled** — and two terminal transitions cannot both run. Recording the money and stamping the
cancellation is therefore ONE state change, expressed as one aggregate method
(`Order.RefuseByMaker`), per CLAUDE.md §2.2. Recorded here rather than quietly diverging.
